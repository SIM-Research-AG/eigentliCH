# report: decisions index

Every choice the sources left open or disagreed on, and every intentional divergence from the prior art, with
the reason. Sources: the Engine Building Guide, the Engine 15 page, the build brief of 28.09.2026 and the
coordinator's note on lbs of the same day, and the prior art in `Projects/eigentliCH`:
`engines/Life_Balance_Sheet/personal_alm/app/report.py` (`ReportFacts`), `desktop/dossier.py`,
`desktop/prose.py`, `desktop/sectionprose.py`, `Prototype/backend/eigentlich/services/befund.py`.

| REP-01 | The engine lives in `eigentliCH_Engines/engines/report` with the family's shared `.venv`; anatomy after `pcp`. Its spark7 client is its own copy of `chatbot`'s module, not a shared import. | Guide section 1: engines share contracts, not code. |
| REP-02 | `psycopg` is outside the allowlist and admitted. | The Guide mandates PostgreSQL (as pcp PCP-02). |
| REP-03 | The code owns every figure: a report states a figure only as a `Fact` read from an artefact, with engine, artefact id, contract and JSON pointer, or derived by the code (a count, a change) naming its derivation and every source. The model is handed one section's facts and its prose is checked against those only: every number of at least two digits must be one of the enumerated renderings of a section fact (as `chatbot` CHB-07, plus percentage points for a change in a share and the year of a date fact). | Prior art `befund.py` (every sentence a fact with provenance; the model may rephrase), `sectionprose.py` (per-section check: the observed failure is a true figure under the wrong label, and a narrow allowed set catches more of it than a report-wide one). |
| REP-04 | Host rule as `chatbot` CHB-04: external providers refused by name, other hosts must be loopback or `*.minimind.ch`, at start. | The brief. |
| REP-05 | Prose slots are a closed list in the calibration; a section without one gets none (household, grid, human capital, risk profile, building blocks, limits, sources). A draft is rejected when it recommends, names another currency, repeats the instruction, is in capitals, leaves the language, or is under 12 or over 140 words; two drafts at most. Statuses: `verified` (printed), `flagged` (kept in the artefact with its unverified numbers, not printed, the page and the warnings say so), `rejected`, `unavailable`, `not_requested`, `no_slot`. | `sectionprose.SLOTS` and `_reject`: each rule was earned by an observed draft there (euros, a repeated heading, capitals). A section absent from the list silently gets no prose: the safe direction. "Every number in model prose must match a fact or be flagged" (the brief). |
| REP-06 | Display facts supplied by the caller (a name, a canton) are shown in the header only and never sent to the model; they are cited to the request, whose content hash (`RRQ-...`) is their artefact id. | The model is given engine facts only; the client's own words stay out of the prompt (M74's structural guarantee in `report.py`). The brief asks every figure to trace to an artefact id; for a caller's fact the request is that artefact. |
| REP-07 | spark7 unreachable or unusable: the report is produced without prose, with a warning, and `complete: false`; the first failure stops the prose for the remaining sections. An incomplete report is stored (append-only) but is never the cached answer to its key: a repeat produces the report again, with prose if spark7 is back. A complete report is the cached answer. | The brief ("if spark7 is unreachable the report is still produced without prose, with a warning"). Caching an outage would make it permanent; ten consecutive timeouts would make a reader wait twenty minutes for nothing (`sectionprose.write_all`). |
| REP-08 | Concurrency: identical requests in one process wait on a per-key lock; across processes a partial unique index allows one complete report per key, and the second writer is handed the first's. | The model is not deterministic in general, so two writers may produce different prose; one of them must be the report for that key. |
| REP-09 | lbs: a partial mirror of the final `lbs-balance-sheet@1.0.0` (household, grid, totals, human capital E, N, H, pensions AHV and BVG, couple cap, property, liquidity and retirement findings, risk profile, mandate proposal, gaps). Labels carry words only: a figure inside a label reaches the prompt without being a fact. | The coordinator's note of 28.09.2026. | REP-11 | The idempotency key uses the source artefact ids, not their content: upstream artefacts are content-addressed and append-only, so an id stands for its content; the sha256 of what was received is recorded per source. | A repeat is answered from the store without asking the upstream engines, so a report stays readable when they are down. |

The brief: "the update states what changed since". |
| REP-13 | The page: the dossiers' stylesheet verbatim (without the form elements of its interactive stage), inlined; every value printed as a `data-fact` element, identifiers and versions in `<code>`, engine notes in a `data-meta` box, only verified prose printed (`data-prose`).
