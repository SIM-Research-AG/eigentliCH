What the report engine does

It turns the engines' published results into one page a client reads: their balance sheet, pensions, goals, the proposed mandate, the allocation and, with lbsim, the outlook. The engine owns every figure and the order of the sections; nothing on the page is typed by hand.

  • Deterministic figures and structure: every figure is a fact read from a published artefact (pcp's allocation, lbs's life balance sheet, lbsim's findings, paths and plan) at a stated place in it, or derived by the code with its derivation named. The sections come in a fixed order and are left out when empty.
  • MiniMind writes only sentences: the house's AI is given one section's facts at a time and writes two or three connecting sentences. Every number in them must be one of that section's facts, or the sentence is withheld. Without MiniMind the report still stands, without prose.
  • Facts and sources: every printed value is a fact (also inside the graphs, which are drawn in the page with no tick numbers); the last section names each source in words and dates. No id is ever printed.
  • Revisions and updates: an update states what changed since the previous report first; a revision carries the curator's remark under the lede.
  • The basis: nominal by default, or real (in today's francs), stated in the lede and next to every return and goal figure. The capitals have no basis: they are model levels, not money.

A client's stored reports

A stored report is shown as it was rendered when it was made; a report made before engine 1.5.0 has no life balance sheet and no capitals sections. "Produce again" asks for the same report under the running engine.
