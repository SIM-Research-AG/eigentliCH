# chatbot: decisions index

Every choice the sources left open or disagreed on, and every intentional divergence from the prior art, with
the reason. Sources: the Engine Building Guide, the Engine 16 page, the build brief of 28.09.2026, the prior art
in `Projects/eigentliCH/Prototype/backend/eigentlich/services` (`know.py`, `compose.py`, `prose.py`,
`router.py`, `grounding.py`, `../boundary.py`) and `Projects/eigentliCH/desktop/coach.py`, and the OGD Plattform's
inference configuration (`Information_Governance/OGD_Plattform/api/config/inferenz.yaml`,
`api/src/app/inferenz/konfiguration.py`).

| Id | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| CHB-01 | The engine lives in `eigentliCH_Engines/engines/chatbot` with the family's shared `.venv`; anatomy, settings, store, service and API follow `pcp`. The model client is this engine's own module (`spark7.py`); `report` has its own copy, no shared import. | Guide section 1 and the brief: engines share contracts, not code. |
| CHB-02 | `psycopg` is outside the allowlist and admitted. | The Guide mandates PostgreSQL; a driver is unavoidable (as pcp PCP-02). |
| CHB-03 | Stateless with respect to client data: the caller sends the grounding (approved notes it chose) and the client facts it allows; the engine never reads the consumer store and has no retrieval of its own. The store keeps what was asked and what was answered. | The brief. It also makes the prior art's M74 guarantee structural: what is not in the request cannot reach the answer. Retrieval (`grounding.py`, `know.retrieve`) is the caller's. |
| CHB-04 | Host rule, checked when the configuration is loaded (so at start) and again when the client is built: a known external generative-AI provider is refused by name (the OGD Plattform's list plus `claude.ai`); any other host must be loopback or `*.minimind.ch`; plain http is accepted for loopback only. Matching is on the host, exact or as a domain suffix, never a substring. | The brief ("loopback or the house's own hosts, never an external provider"). Stricter than the OGD Plattform, whose rule is a blocklist only: an allowlist cannot be passed by a provider nobody listed. |
| CHB-05 | spark7 is spoken to in the OpenAI-compatible form only, and every call streams (SSE). The read timeout (`first_byte_s`, 120 s) bounds the first byte and every silence after it, `total_s` (300 s) the whole call. `Cache-Control: no-cache` on every request. The token headers are configured by variable name; values come from the environment or the family `.env`, parsed in `settings.py` (python-dotenv is not on the allowlist), held with `repr=False`. An answer whose echoed model differs from the configured one is refused. Failures are *unavailable* (retry later) or *unusable* (retrying will not help). | Measured on spark7, 28.09.2026: vLLM, `/v1/models` and `/v1/chat/completions` answer, Ollama routes 404; the access proxy cuts at 125 s before the first byte. The OGD client's OpenAI form did not stream and names that as an open cliff. |
| CHB-06 | Idempotency with a model that is not deterministic: the key is the request's content hash, the calibration hash, the model name and host, the prompt version and hash, and the engine and contract versions. The first answer stored under a key is the answer for that key: a repeat is served from the store without a model call. Identical concurrent requests in one process wait on a per-key lock; across processes the unique key decides, and the second writer is handed the first's answer. A failed run (spark7 unavailable, no usable draft) stores no answer and is not cached; asking again calls the model again. | The brief ("key on inputs + model + prompt version, store the output, return the stored artefact on repeat"). A cached failure would turn an outage into a permanent non-answer. |
| CHB-07 | The number check: every number of at least two digits in the answer must match a source it was allowed to use: the grounding texts, the numeric client facts (only when they reached the prompt), the question, and the client's own earlier turns (never an earlier *answer*). A match is one of an enumerated set of renderings, never a tolerance: as written, rounded to 0, 1 or 2 decimals (half up and half even), scaled only with a scale word written in the answer, as a percentage only with a percent sign and only for a share. Dates are recognised first and matched as dates. Unmatched numbers are listed, not hidden and not blocking: the answer carries them in `unverified_numbers` and in its warnings, and the caller decides whether to show it. | Prior art `desktop/prose.py` (readings of ambiguous Swiss spellings, list separators), `know.unverifiable_figures` (a figure must match a figure; two digits, so labels like "Säule 3a" pass), `compose.audit` (recorded, not a gate, after the owner reversed C-11). Measured while building: a free scaled match let a bare "10" verify against 9 500 000, which is why scale and percent need their word or sign. It catches fabrication, not misattribution. |
| CHB-08 | Route: the client facts reach the prompt only for a question about the client (first-person markers, German and English, from `boundary.asks_about_the_member`); the answer records the route, whether the facts were used, and warns when facts were sent and withheld. | The prior art keeps this rule as data protection (C-04, C-05), not compliance: a question about the world is answered without the client's figures. Generous by design, the costs are not symmetric. |
| CHB-09 | *(Superseded by CHB-18 and CHB-19 on 29.09.2026; kept for answers stored under calibration 1.0.0.)* Refusal: an empty grounding is refused without a model call (`no_grounding`); a model reply `covered: false` is refused (`not_covered`). The reader sees the calibration's fixed text in the request's language, never the model's words. Both refusals are answers and are stored. | The brief ("refused + reason when the grounding does not cover the question"); the prior art's `nothing_found` and `nothing_answers` notices. A fixed text cannot carry an invented figure. |
| CHB-10 | The model answers under vLLM guided decoding (`response_format: json_schema`, `{covered, answer, cited}`). A reply that is not that object, or that contains characters of another script, is redrafted, at most `generation.attempts` (2) drafts, the second with a language reminder; then the run fails (`/answer` 502). | Measured on spark7: the schema is honoured, 1.3 to 4.3 s per answer. The prior art (`compose.drifted`) measured a model switching to Chinese mid-answer; one retry, then no answer rather than a broken one. |
| CHB-11 | Citations are the ids the model lists in `cited`, plus inline `[id]` marks of known notes, which are removed from the text (their digits would be read as figures); an id that names no given note is dropped with a warning. | The prior art numbers passages and asks for marks (`compose._markers`); an unknown id is a citation of nothing. |
| CHB-12 | Text repairs, never a gate: Markdown presentation is removed; German `ß` becomes `ss` and the informal forms a word swap can repair become polite (`compose.siezen`; a remaining `du` is reported). No advice-phrase filter: the prompt forbids recommendations and every answer carries the notice. | Prior art `prose.for_member`, `coach.swiss_ss`, `compose.siezen`. The phrase gate C-01 was withdrawn by the owner (A160, A164): a blacklist of phrasings does not converge. |
| CHB-13 | Limits are refused, not truncated: at most 12 notes, 6 000 characters a note, 40 000 in all, 40 client facts, the last 6 turns of history, 2 000 characters a turn (calibration); beyond that 422. | A cut note is a different note, and the answer would cite text the model never saw. The context is 32 768 tokens. |
| CHB-14 | Calibration 1.0.0: temperature 0, seed 7, 900 output tokens, 2 drafts, the limits above, the number check above, the German and English client markers and refusal texts. | Description from sources, not composition (`desktop/prose.py`: a creative model invents figures). gemma 4 on vLLM has no reasoning preamble (measured: a short answer in 0.3 s), so 900 tokens is ample for 200 words in JSON. |
| CHB-15 | A warm-up tick (one token every 240 s) keeps the model loaded; off in the tests and in `init-db`; its last outcome is on `/health` and `/meta`. | The OGD Plattform measured a cold load of about 65 s against 4 s warm; a failed tick is normal on a shared server. |
| CHB-16 | Languages: German and English. | The brief. French and Italian (the prior art's four) are an open point. |
| CHB-17 | Golden: five requests, spark7's replies frozen once from the live server (`dev/build_golden.py --live`), replayed by the stand-in; the engine's handling of each is pinned exactly. One opt-in live test (`-m live`). | Guide section 6.1; the model output is the one thing a stand-in cannot invent honestly. |
| CHB-18 | **General answers** (owner, 29.09.2026): no refusal for lack of grounding. The model returns two parts, `from_notes` (only the notes and the client facts, cited) and `general` (general knowledge, practical steps the client can take, using the client facts when the question is about the client). The code, not the model, composes the reader's text: the part from the notes, then the general part under the calibration's fixed line ("Allgemeine Einschätzung von MiniMind, nicht aus den geprüften Unterlagen:" / "General assessment by MiniMind, not drawn from the approved notes:"). A part given as from the notes that cites no given note is shown as general. `basis` records `grounded`, `general` or `mixed`. Unchanged: every number checked against grounding, client facts, question and the client's own turns, unmatched ones listed (the app shows them flagged); the prompt forbids any number not in those sources and any figure about the client not in the client facts; no arithmetic; the notice. | The owner: "we must be able to answer such questions", after a client asking how to build a personal growth strategy to earn more received the refusal text because the app's retrieval found no note. A fixed marking line cannot be left out or reworded by the model, so a general statement cannot pass as an approved one. |
| CHB-19 | *(The domain definition in (c) was widened by CHB-23.)* **The remaining refusals**, narrowly: (a) a question with no word in it (no run of two letters, such as "???") is refused without a model call; (b) the model's `status` `unintelligible`; (c) its `status` `out_of_domain`, defined as a question with nothing to do with money, income, work and career, saving, pensions, insurance, taxes, housing and home ownership, debt, investing, the economy or eigentliCH (a recipe, a sports result, programming help, a medical diagnosis). Personal financial questions are always in the domain. Each shows a fixed calibration text naming MiniMind. An empty question stays a 422 (contract). All three carry `refused: true`, `refusal_code: not_covered`, and a `refusal_reason` starting `unintelligible:` or `out_of_domain:`. | The owner: refuse only an empty or unintelligible question or one outside the domain entirely. No new refusal code, so the app's mirror of `chat-answer@1.0.0` keeps validating (CHB-20). |
| CHB-20 | **Contract compatibility**: `chat-answer@1.0.0` keeps its version and every field; two optional fields are added, `basis` (`grounded`, `general`, `mixed`; `null` on a refusal and on answers stored before) and `model_display_name` (`MiniMind`). `refusal_code` keeps its two values; `no_grounding` is no longer produced. `notice` now reads "Composed by MiniMind. Model-derived research output. Not investment advice." (it still ends as before). `chat-request@1.0.0` is unchanged. | The consumer app mirrors the contract; additive optional fields and unchanged enumerations are the compatible change. A mirror that forbids extra fields must add the two. |
| CHB-21 | **MiniMind**: the house's AI is called MiniMind; spark7 is only the server it runs on. `model.display_name: MiniMind` in `config.yaml` is the name readers see: in the answer's marking line, the refusal texts (`{assistant}` in the calibration), the notice, `model_display_name`, the system prompt ("Sie sind MiniMind, die KI von eigentliCH"), `/health`, `/meta` and the test bench. `service: spark7`, the host `spark7.minimind.ch`, the `SPARK7_*` variables and the technical `model` field are unchanged. The display name enters the prompt hash and so the idempotency key. | The owner, 29.09.2026. Configured rather than written into the code, so a rename is a configuration change and a new key, not a silent reuse of stored answers. |
| CHB-22 | **Calibration 1.1.0, prompt `chatbot-prompt@1.1.0`, engine 1.1.0**: 1 200 output tokens (answers of up to 250 words in two parts), the general texts above; otherwise 1.0.0. Calibration 1.0.0 stays in the store as history (its bytes and hash unchanged: `general` is left out of the canonical form when absent) and a request naming it is a 422. Golden re-frozen from spark7 on 29.09.2026 with nine cases (the growth-strategy question, a mixed answer, out of domain, without a word, among them). | Guide 6.1: a prompt change is re-frozen from the real server. An old calibration run under the new prompt would refuse nothing and mark nothing correctly. |
| CHB-23 | **The wider domain, prompt `chatbot-prompt@1.2.0`, engine 1.2.0** (29.09.2026): rule 4 of the prompt now names the domain as personal finances (money, budget, saving, debt, investing), work, career and income, pensions and retirement, insurance, taxes, housing and home ownership, the law around family, marriage, estate and incapacity (inheritance law, compulsory shares, advances on inheritance, wills, Vorsorgeauftrag, Patientenverfügung), care for relatives and its costs (Betreuungsgutschriften, Entlastung, nursing homes), health as it bears on work, income and money, the economy and eigentliCH itself; and it says that notes which do not cover a question never make it out of domain. Out of domain stays narrow: a recipe, a sports result, programming help, a medical diagnosis. Calibration 1.1.0 is unchanged (its refusal text is shown only on a refusal). Golden re-frozen from spark7 with thirteen cases: the three requests the consumer app sent while building the use cases (notes and client facts as sent) and a medical-diagnosis case added. | Measured: the three requests (care for a parent, incapacity, inheritance) were refused as `out_of_domain` under prompt 1.1.0 when replayed against spark7 with the notes the app had chosen (none of which covered them), and answered without notes; the model read "the notes do not cover it" as "outside the domain". Under 1.2.0 all three are answered as general assessments with no unverified number, and the recipe, sports, programming and chest-pain questions are still refused. The prompt version and the engine version enter the key, so no stored refusal is served for them again. |
| CHB-24 | **The schema is applied under a lock** (03.10.2026, `Engines/deploy/ENGINE_CHANGES.md` item 3, as lbsim LBSIM-20). `Store.initialise()` takes `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('chatbot.schema'))` (`store.SCHEMA_LOCK`) as the first statement of the transaction that applies `schema.sql`, so processes starting at the same moment queue instead of deadlocking on `pg_proc` or colliding on `CREATE SCHEMA IF NOT EXISTS`; the commit or rollback releases it. `tests/test_schema_lock.py`: 4 threads with their own connections apply it at once, 5 rounds on a throwaway schema, none fails; `initialise()` waits while another session holds the lock. | Every process applies the schema at start-up; lbsim saw the deadlock when three workers started together (01.10.2026), and the chatbot has the same pattern. |
| CHB-25 | **`/health` out of uvicorn's access log** (03.10.2026, item 10). `python -m chatbot serve` puts a logging filter on `uvicorn.access` (`__main__.quiet_health_probes`) that drops the records whose path is `/health`; every other request is still logged. No engine version change: the engine version enters the key (CHB-23) and neither change moves an answer, so `chatbot@1.2.0` stays. | The container's health check calls every engine every 30 seconds, which buried the lines that matter. A filter needs no new dependency and no setting. |
