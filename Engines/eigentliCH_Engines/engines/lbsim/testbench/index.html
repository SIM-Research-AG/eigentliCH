What lbsim does

lbsim reads a household's Life Balance Sheet from lbs and says what it means over time. For one sheet it computes:

  1. Findings: the deterministic checks on the sheet (thin liquidity, hours above the model's threshold, a goal the household cannot fund at a return of zero, and so on), each with the figures behind it.
  2. Earning power: what the principal could earn at a full-time week, from the stated income or the model.
  3. Income paths: the principal's gross income year by year on a few paths (as today, after an education, at a full working week), and what each goal needs saved a year on each path.
  4. The Monte Carlo: the household under its stated plan, simulated a few thousand times on the client's allocation in the current market assessment and in each scenario (the Regimes). It gives the range of wealth year by year, the chance of reaching each goal, and how expertise, network and health develop.
  5. The plan calculation: in the background (up to two hours) an optimiser looks for the working time, learning, spending and saving that reach the goal; the bench shows what it assumes for this period.

Pick a client below. The list holds each client's newest sheet that lbsim has computed, labelled by the goal and the sheet's date. "Run" recomputes the findings and the paths without asking for a plan calculation, but it replaces any plan calculation still queued or running for that client.
