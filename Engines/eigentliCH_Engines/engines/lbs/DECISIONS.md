# lbs: decisions index

Every choice the sources left open or disagreed on, and every intentional divergence from the prior art, with
the reason. Sources, ranked: the Engine Building Guide, the owner's ruling of 28.09.2026 (below), the eigentliCH
prototype's services (`Projects/eigentliCH/Prototype/backend/eigentlich/services/`) and content records
(`Prototype/client/content/`), the eigentliCH draft mandate derivation (`Projects/eigentliCH/engines/lbs/derive.py`,
`contracts/snapshot.py`) and the Optimizer's input contract (`pcp-mandate@1.0.0`).

**Owner's decisions, 29.09.2026** (LBS-23 to LBS-27): approve `ahv-pension` and `risk-profile`; correct the four
quirks of LBS-17 in a new engine version and calibration, keeping the prototype golden as the reference for the
reproduction mode; the consumer app's yearly saving feeds the mandate's contribution; the new calibration is the
default and the mirrored contracts stay unchanged. Second round (LBS-28 to LBS-30): the three behaviours left
open by LBS-24 and LBS-25 are corrected in calibration 1.3.0 (lbs@1.2.0): a human-capital stock is never free
wealth, a stated mortgage of 0 is a paid-off mortgage, and the yearly saving is split between goals by a stated
share per goal.

**Owner's ruling, 28.09.2026:** `lbs@1.0.0` is the deterministic life balance sheet built from the prototype's
services, with their figures as the golden reference. The stochastic control model `personal_alm`
(`Projects/eigentliCH/engines/Life_Balance_Sheet`) is not used; it is left for `lbsim`.
