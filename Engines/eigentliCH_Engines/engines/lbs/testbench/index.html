What lbs does

lbs turns one household's stated facts into its life balance sheet: a deterministic calculation, the same request always gives the same sheet, and nothing on it is guessed. Every input it could not use is named as a gap, never filled in.

  • Financial assets by vessel: what is freely available, in the second pillar, in pillar 3a, and in real assets such as a home.
  • Human capital: per adult the three capitals beside wealth, expertise and education (E), network (N) and health (H), each a model level on its own scale, not money.
  • Liabilities and goals: what the household owes and what its goals will claim, against what it holds, with the net worth.
  • Pensions and findings: the AHV and BVG pensions, and whether a home purchase, liquidity and retirement hold.
  • The mandate proposal: the return a goal needs, in the shape the portfolio engine (pcp) reads, for a curator to complete. Unreleased by design.
  • The real view: goal amounts and returns in nominal francs or in today's francs (real), with the inflation assumption stated. Use the switch below.

Pick a use-case client to load the request lbs stored for its newest sheet and show that sheet. Changing the request and pressing Run makes a new sheet.

Request (lbs-request@1.0.0): the client's facts as JSON, filled by the picker
