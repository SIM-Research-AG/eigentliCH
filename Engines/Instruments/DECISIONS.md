# fmre: decisions index

Choices the sources left open, and deliberate departures, with the reason. Earlier
decisions of this engine are recorded where they were taken (module docstrings, README
"Three places this build knowingly departs from the manual", HANDOVER section 4). This
index starts with the review round of 28 September 2026 (R-001, R-002, R-003) and the
cross-engine owner decisions D-01 (currency) and D-02 (horizon) of the same day.

**FMRE-01 to FMRE-14 change nothing that is published**: every behaviour they add is
opt-in. **FMRE-15 to FMRE-20 are the owner's decisions of 29 September 2026 and move the
published default on purpose** (FMRE-21, the same day, adds a provenance field for pcp): Income becomes real estate 75 % and equity 25 % (new
calibration `CAL-69d9d1ee5245ac71`), two instruments change or confirm role, two are
deactivated, and Long Volatility Index reads a new price proxy. **FMRE-22 to FMRE-24, later
the same day, decide R-002**: the default estimator is the 12 month forward measurement
with a light smoothing across neighbouring states (`forward_12m_smoothed`), the estimator
and the currency enter every `return_set_id`, and each protection instrument is judged by
the rule of its type. `GET /v1/return-set` now returns `RS-8b6a98484992c9d3` unstamped and
`RS-59598b143ab78c56` stamped against `RGM-e2658e8e9bbbc81e` (before: `RS-ca414ad663de0621`,
`RS-101354ae344db5b3`, and before FMRE-20 `RS-b61c78223f520245`, `RS-9cf5467a868babbe`),
contract `rs@1.0.0`, pinned by `tests/test_default_pin.py`.

**FMRE-26 to FMRE-32 build the nominal and real view** (owner, 29 September 2026, `review/REAL_VIEW_INTERFACES.md`): `GET /v1/inflation`, an opt-in `basis=real` on the ReturnSet and the instrument profile, the ceiling and the hard-currency fallback, the scenario deflator from aggregation. The nominal default keeps its ids and bytes; the one deliberate byte change is `provenance.estimator` (FMRE-26).

**FMRE-33 to FMRE-37 build the inflation pass-through (beta) under a scenario Regime** (owner, 29 September 2026): a scenario set carries each instrument from its historical real return to the scenario's inflation by a house beta per instrument type (a versioned calibration, `ipt@1.0.0`) or the CIO's override, in `basis=nominal` and `basis=real`. Base Regimes, the nominal default and `/v1/inflation` are unchanged byte for byte; every scenario set's id moves.

**FMRE-38 to FMRE-41 are the owner's decisions on the pass-through** (29 September 2026, calibration `ipt@1.1.0`, `IPT-585c7da4656ead2b`; `ipt@1.0.0` stays stored): the bond loss in log form without a cap, cash beta 0, the four role profiles carried by the weighted beta of their blocks, and the judgement calls of the register mapping confirmed. Base Regimes, the nominal default and `/v1/inflation` are unchanged byte for byte; every scenario set's id moves again.
