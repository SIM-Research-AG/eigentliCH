# fmre: decisions index

Choices the sources left open, and deliberate departures, with the reason. Earlier
decisions of this engine are recorded where they were taken (module docstrings, README
"Three places this build knowingly departs from the manual", HANDOVER section 4). This
index starts with the review round of 28 September 2026 (R-001, R-002, R-003) and the
cross-engine owner decisions D-01 (currency) and D-02 (horizon) of the same day.

**FMRE-01 to FMRE-14 change nothing that is published**: every behaviour they add is
opt-in. **FMRE-15 to FMRE-20 are the owner's decisions of 29 September 2026 and move the
published default on purpose** (FMRE-21, the same day, adds a provenance field for pcp): Income becomes real estate 75 % and equity 25 % (new
calibration `CAL-69d9d1ee5245ac71`), two instruments change or confirm role, two are
deactivated, and Long Volatility Index reads a new price proxy. **FMRE-22 to FMRE-24, later
the same day, decide R-002**: the default estimator is the 12 month forward measurement
with a light smoothing across neighbouring states (`forward_12m_smoothed`), the estimator
and the currency enter every `return_set_id`, and each protection instrument is judged by
the rule of its type. `GET /v1/return-set` now returns `RS-8b6a98484992c9d3` unstamped and
`RS-59598b143ab78c56` stamped against `RGM-e2658e8e9bbbc81e` (before: `RS-ca414ad663de0621`,
`RS-101354ae344db5b3`, and before FMRE-20 `RS-b61c78223f520245`, `RS-9cf5467a868babbe`),
contract `rs@1.0.0`, pinned by `tests/test_default_pin.py`.

| Id | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| FMRE-01 | **12 month forward measurement** (`ProfileMethod.FORWARD_12M`, `engines/fund_map/forward.py`). Each month with a signal is tagged with its calibration state; the window is the 12 months `t+1 .. t+12`; its value is the sum of the 12 monthly log returns (an annual log return). A window with any month missing is dropped, never filled. | D-02: instrument returns in a state are measured over the following 12 months, the horizon of the role calibration. The window starts after the tagged month so that no month's own return is counted as its own forecast. R-003's diagnosis: the same crisis months give gold -12.51 % over the month and a double-digit gain over the following year. |
| FMRE-02 | **Overlapping monthly-start windows, honest count.** Windows start every month. `n_obs` is the number of distinct months the state's windows cover, divided by 12, rounded down, and never more than the number of windows. The sufficiency floor of six applies to that count; the 20 % trim applies from 20. | R-003 step 1. For one contiguous run this is (months + 11) / 12, about months / 12 as the review asked; for tagged months years apart it approaches one per window, because such windows share no month. Counting overlapping windows as independent would clear the floor on about one year of evidence. |
| FMRE-03 | **Estimated per phase, read onto 25 states by the calibration's own pchip.** Five phase values (crisis .. boom), interpolated on `0.6:0.2:5.4` like the role calibration; states 1, 2, 24, 25 are labelled `extrapolated`, as the role curves' are. | About 240 signal months over 25 states leaves most states without one independent year; over five phases the measured phases carry 6 to 16 effective years (Precious Metals: 10, 6, 14, 16, 5). Built the same way as the role curves, an instrument's curve is comparable with its role's. |
| FMRE-04 | **A phase below the floor is filled from D2's scaled role shape, anchored on the phases this instrument measured**: `value(p) = anchor + k * (role(p) - mean(role))`, `k` from D2, `anchor` so that the fill agrees on average with the measured phases. Labelled `shape-scaled` across the phase's band of five states, `n_obs` = D2's observations. Without a D2 fit (under 24 months) the phase is seeded from the role and labelled `seed`. | The fill keeps the instrument's own level and amplitude and takes only the difference between phases from the role; a plain role seed would put a role-level number next to measured ones (the flat -5 % of R-003 was such a fill). Labelling the whole band, not just the knot, keeps an interpolation between a measured and a filled knot from reading as measured. |
| FMRE-05 | **Series choice for the forward measurement**: the instrument's stored history, or its register proxy's public series from the feed (grade not `none`), whichever gives more complete 12 month windows; ties go to the stored history. Reported on every result (`series`, `series_currency`). | A forward window needs 12 consecutive months. The andersCH recoveries exist only in months the position was held (Precious Metals: 147 of 239), so most windows are incomplete; the proxy is also the unconditional asset, which removes the selection bias README names. Precious Metals therefore reads GLD. The cascade and D2 keep reading the stored history, unchanged. |
| FMRE-06 | **Currency at the point of use (D-01)**, `engines/fund_map/currency.py`. Series stay nominal in their source currency; on request they are converted **monthly, before estimation**, `r_target = (1 + r_source) * X(t) / X(t-1) - 1`, and the profile is measured in the chosen currency. Source currency comes from the source label: `yahoo:*` USD, `andersch-report:*` CHF, otherwise the register currency. A month without both rates is dropped. | A state profile cannot be converted after the fact: the exchange rate moved differently in each state, and one drift added to every state would misstate what a profile exists to show. The register's `currency` column says what the instrument is priced in, not what the stored history is in. |
| FMRE-07 | **FX source**: `fx.USDCHF` (Yahoo `CHF=X`) and `fx.EURCHF` (`EURCHF=X`), month-end, in `fmre_feed`, loaded by `python -m store.etl.fx` (a separate command, like the proxy download; the bootstrap stays offline). They reach 2003; the role curves (US long record, USD, 1870-2020) are **not** converted and say so. | Two rates span CHF, EUR and USD. The earliest signal month is 2006-08, so 2003 covers every estimate. Converting the role curves needs rates back to 1870 (JST Macrohistory R6, HANDOVER section 5), which are not loaded. |
| FMRE-08 | **Opt-in API.** `/v1/return-set?profile_method=&currency=`, `/v1/instruments/{id}/profile?method=&currency=`, `/v1/diagnostics/estimators?currency=`. Nothing computed on request is stored. | D-01: the currency is part of the idempotency key. An opt-in set must never travel under the default's id, and the default's id, notes and run id must not move for pcp, which pins them. |

| FMRE-22 | **R-002 decided: the default estimator is the 12 month forward measurement, lightly smoothed** (`ProfileMethod.FORWARD_12M_SMOOTHED`, `service.DEFAULT_PROFILE_METHOD`; owner, 29.09.2026). It serves the ReturnSet, `/v1/instruments/{id}/profile` and the diagnostics (`coverage`, `estimators`), in every currency, computed on request and never stored. The smoothing is its own step, `forward.smooth_forward` on top of FMRE-01..05: two passes of the binomial kernel (1/4, 1/2, 1/4) over the 25 states, end states padded with themselves; **no phase value (states 3, 8, 13, 18, 23) moves by more than 1 percentage point** (`SMOOTHING_TOLERANCE`; the excess is taken back and the correction spread linearly between knots); **no state changes sign** (a state the kernel would carry across zero keeps its unsmoothed value). Label `forward-12m-smoothed` (added to `Method`, between `shape-scaled` and `extrapolated` in strength, and to `MethodLabel`) on states that rest on the instrument's own measurement, with `n_obs` = the effective years of the phase band; a filled band keeps `shape-scaled` or `seed`; a profile with nothing measured is the role seed, unsmoothed. The unsmoothed profile and the five measured phase values stay on every view (`unsmoothed`, `phases`, `smoothing.knot_shift`). The stored `instrument_profile` rows stay the cascade's (`STORED_PROFILE_METHOD`): they are the cascade's peer basis, and `profile_method=cascade` / `method=cascade` read them back. Measured on the live store, 40 active instruments with history, forward before and after smoothing: mean jump between neighbouring states 1.64 -> 1.49 % (source currency), 1.40 -> 1.28 % (CHF), 1.45 -> 1.32 % (EUR), 1.66 -> 1.51 % (USD); largest step, averaged over instruments, 4.15 -> 3.52 % (source); largest change to a measured phase value 1.00 point (the tolerance binds at 8 of 200 knots, the sharp extrema of Mining Equities, Digital Assets and a few others); 7 to 13 of 1,000 states held for sign; protection check unchanged by the smoothing (same instruments pass and fail as unsmoothed). Cascade for comparison: 9.13 %. | The owner chose smooth profiles in which gold is strong in crisis for every chart and for pcp. The forward measurement already fixes the sign (R-003); the pchip through five phase values has its extrema at the knots, so a light smoother mostly rounds the steepest stretches between them and the extrapolated tails. The mean step is bounded by the distance between the measured phase values, which the tolerance keeps: a smoother that flattened them would be replacing measurement with opinion. Two passes are the lightest kernel that visibly lowers the largest step (one pass: 4.15 -> 3.76 %; four: 3.27 % at the cost of 20 sign holds); one point is small against the phase values' spread and still lets the kernel act on most knots. |
