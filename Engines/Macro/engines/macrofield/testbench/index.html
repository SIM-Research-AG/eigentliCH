How to read this

  • Saturation is BIS total credit to the non-financial sector over GDP, times the credit uplift of the calibration. Phase cut-offs: Foundation below 1.0, Saturation at 3.5 and above; balanced band 2.5 to 3.5.
  • Phase 4 (Saturation and reordering) also fires when K_R/K_I drops below 1. Once an economy is in Phase 4 it stays there until saturation falls below the Foundation level (the latch). With calibration 1.1.0 the latch runs over the whole published history.
  • Fit path: reporting integrates at full accuracy, search only at the looser search tolerance, none means the calibrated equations do not integrate over the window, so there is no simulated path and no projection.
  • Everything here is model-derived research output, not investment advice.
