| AGG-20 | **What a scenario Regime is, and which month represents the scenario.** The same shape as a Regime: the base Regime's dates and history up to its `as_of`, unchanged, then 60 projected month-ends (on the Default Regime: 2026-02-28 to 2031-01-31). Every economy of the base is projected from its own latest assessed distribution (IN and VN from 2024-12, their months 2025-01 to 2026-01 stay unassessed as in the base), so every economy is assessed on every projected month. **Month 60, the target reached, represents the scenario**: it is the Regime's last date, its `provenance.as_of` and `provenance.scenario.scenario_date`, and each economy's and market's `current`; a consumer that reads the latest month where all its economies are assessed (pcp's default, fmre's `/current`) reads it without change. Earlier projected months are the path towards it (month 12 is 2027-01-31 on the Default). On month 60 every economy reads the same distribution, the policy's target, as in the .m file. Markets are blended from the projected economies with the base's weights, by the base's rule (AGG-11). Projected months carry no macro year, blend weights or market reading (`null`), and `/contributions` on one answers `projected: true` with nothing to decompose. **The optimism level is not applied to the projection**: `optimism_scale` stays the base's and shifts only the history; the target is the policy's own reading (Rogue +13.60 states would turn a depression into a boom). A scenario of a scenario Regime is refused (422). | Owner's brief of 29.09.2026 (pcp optimises "under depression" at the month it picks by default). Alternative rejected: only the 60 projected months, which would lose the history and the start the path was made from. |
