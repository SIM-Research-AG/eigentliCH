# mrs: decisions index

Every intentional divergence from the MATLAB Master Controller, and every choice the spec left
open, with the reason. MATLAB sources: `SIM_Tech/Master_Controller/Functions/Market Risk/
Market_Signal.m`, `MR_*.m`, `Weights.m`, `Models/`; weights from `Controller_Test.xlsx`, sheet
`Market_Settings` (hardcoded identically in `MRS_Tester.m`).

Status words: **ruled** (the owner decided), **proposed, needs owner ruling** (chosen here as
the most defensible option; in force until ruled), **lapsed** (superseded; kept for the record).

| Id | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| MRS-01 | Bins are half-open: a reading exactly on grid point k belongs to the bin it opens. MATLAB puts an interior or top-edge reading in column 1 and assigns nothing on the bottom edge. | The MATLAB branch structure makes an on-edge reading jump to the most aggressive column (or leave a stale value). Off the edges the two agree exactly; `test_matlab.py` pins both the agreement and the divergence. Measure zero on continuous data. |
| MRS-02 | Published distributions are normalised to 1; the pre-normalisation total is kept as `raw_mass`, so `distribution * raw_mass` is MATLAB's `CRS` row exactly. | The stress tail adds mass (up to 6^sqrt(2)/11 x its weight), so MATLAB rows do not sum to 1. The spec requires they do; keeping `raw_mass` makes the change lossless. |
| MRS-03 | **Lapsed (MRS-18): optimism moved to `aggregation`.** `optimism_scale` was one of `defensive`, `default`, `aggressive`, `rogue` (edge shifts 0, 0.4, 0.8, 1.2). No numeric values. | The admissible range asked for on the engine page: `Weights.m` defines exactly these four levels. The shifts are calibration; the level is a run parameter and part of the idempotency key. |
| MRS-04 | A missing segment reading is dropped for that date and the present segments re-weighted to the full total; a date needs 3 of 4 segments (`missing_policy: reweight`, `min_segments: 3`). Calibration `0.1.0-matlab` reproduces MATLAB (a NaN selects column 1); `fail` is also available. | Fail loudly. MATLAB's NaN comparisons all come out false, so a gap reads as column 1, the most aggressive band. Kept only for reconciliation. |

## Open points

1. **The stress tail is always on at zero shift.** The stress segment is non-negative by
   construction (flags, and `2*abs` in monetary uncertainty), and at zero shift a reading of
   0 already opens column 6. In the production artefact of 28.09.2026 the tail is on in 100%
   of assessed months; the median modal state is 8 and a quarter of months read state 1.
   It was ruled "keep" on 27.09.2026, on the premise that a reading of 0 switches it on; the
   owner should know it is always on. Under MATLAB's "Default" (+0.4) a reading below 0.4
   kept it off. Where optimism is applied (`aggregation`), this is the lever.
2. MRS-17, MRS-19, MRS-22 and MRS-23 were accepted as proposed by the owner on 28.09.2026.
3. **Signal orientation.** A higher reading in any segment selects a more cautious column;
   the published segments keep MATLAB's orientation (z-scored band segments, un-normalised
   stress sum).
4. **Golden against the local pull.** No MATLAB output exists for datafeed's own input (the
   pull of 05.01.2026). A MATLAB run of `MRS_Tester.m` on `M_TS.mat` would pin that end to
   end; today the `matlab` mode is only shown to run on it.
5. **Data quality** (Notion task "Data quality: market inputs that are empty or cannot work"):
   Germany and Spain bank index negative in the September pull (MRS-21); high-yield yield to
   worst only for US, EU and CN; BIS NEER missing for BD and VN; the BD, JP, MY, PH, TH and VN
   bank index and nine senior-loan series are placeholders.
