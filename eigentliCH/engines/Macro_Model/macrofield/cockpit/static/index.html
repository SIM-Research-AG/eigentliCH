The primary criteria. The model is judged on whether it turns when the economy turns, and on direction, before its level. A coin toss scores 0.50 on direction.

Control board

Every lever is a query parameter, so a board state is a shareable link and a reproducible run. The two policy levers act forward, on the projected path, because a stimulus programme is something done from a future date. They take the form mode:base>target@start-end, for example pulse:1.0>2.0@2027-2032.

        Stock to gold

        The three-driver equation of book chapter 24.2. Driver one is a liquidity impulse and supports gold; drivers two and three are innovation-driven growth and the loss of currency backing. The dominant driver selects the asset stance. The ratio is shown as stocks over gold, which is the direction the book's Weimar figure uses, and the reciprocal is stated in the tiles.
